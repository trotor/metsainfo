Metsäinfo shows Finland's open forest resource data (Finnish Forest Centre) and cadastral boundaries (National Land Survey of Finland) on a map. It is intended for forest owners and professionals, including use in the field. The app is in Finnish and the data covers Finland only. No login is needed.

HOW TO TEST
1. Launch the app. The map opens in Central Finland.
2. Type the property ID 592-404-1-32 in the search field and tap "Hae" (Search). The map zooms to the property and the bottom panel shows a summary of its forest stands.
3. Drag the panel handle up, or tap it, to see the tabs Yhteenveto (Summary), Kuviot (Stands) and Ehdotukset (Proposals).
4. Tap a forest stand on the map: the Kuviot tab opens with that stand's details.
5. Location: tap the arrow button under the zoom buttons. Location is used only on the device to show the user's position and which forest stand they are standing on. To see this, simulate a location in Finland, for example 62.2244, 25.0911 (inside property 592-404-1-32).
6. The ⋯ menu shares a link to the property or a CSV file of the stand data via the iOS share sheet.

IN-APP PURCHASE (version 4.0.0)
"Puukaupan hinta-arvio" (fi.inetor.metsainfo.hintaarvio) is a one-time, non-consumable purchase that unlocks the timber price estimate for all properties. The app is distributed in Finland only, but the in-app purchase is available in all storefronts, so it loads with any sandbox account. There is no login and no other restriction.
7. Open property 592-404-1-32 (steps 2-3). On the Yhteenveto (Summary) tab, the card "Arvio nykyhinnoin" shows a locked value "? ??? €" and a button "Avaa" (Open). There is also a fourth tab "Arvio" with the button "Avaa hinta-arvio". Both open the purchase screen, which shows the price from the App Store and has "Osta hinta-arvio" (Buy) and "Palauta aiempi osto" (Restore Purchases).
8. Without purchasing, "Kokeile esimerkkimetsällä" opens a fictional demo forest where the whole feature works: current stumpage prices are fetched with "Hae verkosta", prices can be edited, and the estimate is shown per year.
9. After purchase, the Arvio tab shows the estimate for the real property: the price region is derived from the property's municipality, prices come from the weekly roundwood trade statistics (Finnish Forest Industries Federation, published in the Natural Resources Institute Finland statistics database), and "Jaa arvio" shares the estimate as a CSV file.

DATA AND PRIVACY
The app does not collect any data, has no accounts, ads or analytics. Purchases are handled by the App Store; ownership is checked on the device with StoreKit. Map tiles and forest data are fetched directly from the public open-data interfaces of the Finnish Forest Centre and the National Land Survey (CC BY 4.0); stumpage prices from the Natural Resources Institute Finland statistics database and the property's municipality from Statistics Finland (only the property's reference point is sent). The app is not an official service of these agencies.
